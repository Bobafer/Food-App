# Click&Cook Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Click&Cook helps users identify ingredients from
photos of their refrigerators and discover recipes
using artificial intelligence.

This Privacy Policy explains what information
Click&Cook processes, how it is used, and how
third-party services may be involved.

## 2. Information We Process

The app uses photos you take on the app
and sends it to Google Gemini. The app asks for 
your allergies, food intolerances, and other food 
preferences, which are optional.

## 3. Artificial Intelligence

Click&Cook uses Google Gemini to provide
AI-powered ingredient detection and recipe
suggestions.

The app sends your photo and the food allergies,
intolerances, and preferences that you select
to Google Gemini.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Information in the Meal Plan, Inventory, Recipes,
and Settings pages are stored locally on your device.
