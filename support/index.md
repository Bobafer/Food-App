# ClicktoCook Support

Welcome to Click&Cook Support!

Click&Cook makes cooking easier by helping you
discover recipes using ingredients you already
have at home.

If you encounter any issues, have questions, or
would like to provide feedback, we're here to help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Click&Cook work?

Click&Cook uses artificial intelligence to analyze
photos of your refrigerator, identify visible
ingredients, and suggest recipes based on them.

### Why isn't ingredient detection working?

Make sure your photo is clear, well-lit, and that
your ingredients are visible. Also, ensure that
you have an active internet connection.

### How can I save recipes?

You can save recipes within the app to access
them later. 

### How can I report a bug?

If you experience a technical issue or encounter
unexpected behavior, please contact us using
the email address below.
